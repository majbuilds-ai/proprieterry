ROUTE 01 // FOUNDATION PLAN

Buying my first home

  • Liquidity beats 20% down: Keeping $25k in post-closing reserves with low-cost PMI is often safer than draining cash to hit 20%.
  • Net-pay budgeting: Banks qualify you on gross income; we design your target payment around actual take-home comfort.
  • Seller-funded buydowns: A $15k seller credit drops your rate 2% in Year 1—saving 3× more monthly than a $15k price reduction.
  • 2 years of W-2s or tax returns: Plus 30 days of recent pay stubs (or 24 months of 1099/K-1 history).
  • 60 days of asset statements: Checking, savings, brokerage, or RSU accounts—pause large transfers before applying.
  • Sleep-well monthly ceiling: Your target all-in monthly number and comfortable cash-to-close limit.
